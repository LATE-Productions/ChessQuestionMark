## Working title

Chess AR

## Interaction in one sentence

[Describe the physical action and the digital response in one clear sentence.]

Moving markers physically will augment a reality where chess is played!

## Physical marker action

What will the user do with the marker?
- [For example: rotate it, move it left/right, change its distance, or hide/show it]

User will move markers in 8 different directions depending chess piece, user can also rotate markers to change orientation of marker.
User will also be able to hide/show marker to indicate if chess pieces have been captured.

## Application response

What will change in the application?
- [For example: vehicle steering in Unity, sound parameters, visual properties, or simulation input]

The application will augment a visual representation of a chess board and chess pieces so that the user/users can play chess using visual queues and game logic.

## Minimum demonstrable version

[Describe the smallest interaction that would count as a reliable working result.]

A chess board will be projected in an augmented reality with a unique marker.
This will be combined with atleast 2 markers recognized as chess pieces, 1 marker for each user.
Chess piece markers will be interacted with to play and win chess.

## Optional extension

[Describe one extension to attempt only after the minimum version works.]

An extenstion for the minimum version is for the augmented chess board to have smaller implemented game knowledge.
Meaning chess board will understand where chess pieces are on the board, this can provide with better visual queues, for example showing chess pieces moving options when interacted with.

## Why this interaction?

[Briefly explain why the physical action and digital response make sense together.]

The idea is to be able to play chess anywhere you want using only a camera and markers.
Moving markers will act like moving chess pieces and playing chess in a augmented reality.

Considering gaming and games are popular, adding animations or effects to a augment chess board gives chess a whole new experiance.
